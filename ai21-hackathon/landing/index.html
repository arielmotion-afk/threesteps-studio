FAQ

Do I need to code?

No. Teams need product, research and ops people as much as engineers.

Can I join remotely?

Yes. Kickoff, check-ins and demos are streamed, and remote people join the same teams.

Which models can we use?

The Jamba family through the internal API: Jamba Large 1.7 (jamba-large) and Jamba Mini 2 (jamba-mini). Jamba2 3B is there too as open weights, if your idea needs a small model. Keys are handed out at kickoff.

How much context do we get?

256K tokens on every Jamba model. That's enough for long contracts, full codebases or a year of support tickets in one prompt, so ideas that need a lot of input are welcome.

Can we build in Hebrew?

Yes. Jamba supports 9 languages: English, Hebrew, Arabic, Spanish, French, Portuguese, Italian, Dutch and German.

What if I don't have an idea?

Come anyway. Ideas are posted in #context-window before the event, and most teams form around one of those.

Does it count as work time?

Yes. Both days are cleared with team leads.
