Maestro Launch

Launch campaign · AI21 Labs

The launch campaign for AI21 Maestro, from the launch video to the conference floor to the sales call.

AI21 Maestro plans and runs multi-step AI tasks for enterprise teams. I led motion for the launch: the launch video, the HumanX conference stand and the LinkedIn campaign. The sales deck is the piece I'd add now. Adva Cohen designed the visual language the campaign is built on.

Convention

Two stand designs I drafted for AI21 at HumanX: a corner stand and a curved pop-up wall with a counter. Neither was built because of budget. The booth video plays on the screens in both.

The booth video ran on a loop and tells the whole story without sound, so it reads from across the hall. It ends on a QR code that takes visitors to the Maestro page.

Sales deck

The campaign built awareness, but nothing in it was made for the sales call that follows. This is the deck I'd add: ten slides for a first discovery call, in the classic order. Why now, the problem, the solution, how it works, business outcomes, use cases, how Maestro compares, and a pilot plan with success criteria. It ends on an agreed next step.

Open the deck

LinkedIn content

Four square videos for LinkedIn, each in two versions: Join the waitlist before launch, and Learn more after it. Each one explains a single part of the product in under 45 seconds.

What I learned

The campaign was strong, but sales joined too late to use it. It built awareness, and nothing was ready for the stages after it. Next time I'd start with sales: what reps need on a first call, which objections come up, and what gets a prospect to a pilot. Then I'd build the launch from there.
