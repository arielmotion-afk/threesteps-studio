Why

Six face yoga exercises, a seven-step morning routine, four steps at night. I wanted one app with exactly that: my moves, my products, a timer I can follow with my hands on my face, and a streak. No account, no subscription, works offline.

I directed; Claude Code wrote the code. I tested every change on my phone and decided what stayed.

Motion

Each screen has a sticker character that acts out its moment, animated like stop-motion: the sprout’s leaves flutter, the sun squints, the moon nods off, and Track flips through the six exercise faces as each one does its move.

  • 12 fps, on twosOne shared clock drives every character, so they all step together, like hand-drawn animation.
  • Crisp outlinesAn earlier pass wobbled every edge with a pixel filter. It read as a glitch, so it went.
  • Rare surprisesThe title dances once a session; the squiggle under it redraws every 20 to 40 seconds.
  • An Icon LabA hidden page in the app with fps, slow motion, pause and frame-step, my After Effects timeline for code.
