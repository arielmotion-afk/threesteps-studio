A plain-language tour of fusion, and where our magnets fit in.

01 · Heat

Make a small star

Fusion joins light atoms, the way the sun does. On Earth that takes fuel heated past a hundred million kelvin, hot enough to become plasma, while the magnets that hold it sit at 20 K.

02 · Hold

Hold it without touching it

No material survives that heat, so the plasma is held in place by magnetic fields. In a tokamak, a ring of coils around the plasma makes that field.

Why 20 T

Double the field, sixteen times the power density

In a tokamak, fusion power density rises roughly with the fourth power of the magnetic field. That is why our customers want 20 T, and why we build for it.

03 · Shrink

Stronger field, smaller machine

The stronger the field, the smaller the machine can be for the same output. That is why our customers want 20 tesla, and why we build for it.

A note on accuracy

Simplified on purpose
