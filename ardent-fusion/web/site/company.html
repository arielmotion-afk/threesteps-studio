Our story

Started by people who were tired of waiting for magnets

Our founders spent years building high-field magnets for research. Every fusion team they met had the same problem: the magnets took longest to get. In 2024 they set up Ardent to make that part someone else's job.

We design, wind and test 20 tesla magnet modules, and deliver them to the teams building fusion machines. In 2026 our demo coil held 21.3 T for six hours. Next year the first AR-1 ships.
