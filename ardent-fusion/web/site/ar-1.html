The physics, briefly

Double the field, sixteen times the power density

In a tokamak, fusion power density rises roughly with the fourth power of the magnetic field. A stronger field lets a smaller machine reach the same output.

Where it goes

Eighteen modules, one reactor

Our customers ring eighteen {product} modules around the plasma. Together they make the field that holds it in place. We deliver them one at a time, each tested to full field.
